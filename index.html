About E&S

Malta's Premier Construction Partner

Founded in 2004, E&S Construction has grown from a small local builder to one of Malta's most respected construction companies. We've delivered over 350 projects — from intimate Gozitan farmhouses to large commercial developments in Valletta.

Our 50+ skilled tradesmen, engineers, and project managers are committed to one thing: structures that stand the test of time, crafted with integrity and Maltese pride.
