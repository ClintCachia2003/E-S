Our Story

Malta's Trusted Builder for Over 35 Years

Founded in 1987 and based in Attard, E&S Construction has grown from a family-run building firm into one of Malta's most respected construction companies. We take pride in every project — from a modest extension to a full multi-storey development.

Our expertise spans both modern residential and commercial construction, and traditional Maltese limestone craftsmanship — the hand-cut arches, ornate cornices, and gallarija that define Malta's architectural heritage. Every build carries the same commitment: quality that stands for generations.

Our Services

From foundations to finishing, we handle every stage of your construction project with the same dedication and skill.

Residential Homes

Villas, townhouses, and bungalows built to your exact specifications. We manage everything from permits to the finishing coat.

Apartment Blocks

Traditional Limestone

Hand-crafted Maltese limestone masonry — arches, cornices, gallarija, and ornate facades that honour Malta's architectural heritage.

Renovations

Commercial Buildings
